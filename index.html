What do we do at Hydroxonium?

We provide the ones who haven't had the liberty to be birthed with the right cards the tools in order to be creatively powerful in our current landscape. Creativity shouldn't be locked behind subscription based models. We don't have to submit to needless SaaS models in order to get what we want. The elite create the illness; spread it like Bonnie Blue. Then they sell us our "medicine" in the form of arbitrary subscriptions that serve little to no purpose other than to siphon money out of you like the goblins they are, whilst hiding the true cure; hiding it away from the public until its rarity outmatches jewels with blinding luster.

"We think there is a fundamental misconception about piracy. Piracy is almost always a service problem and not a pricing problem. If a pirate offers a product anywhere in the world, 24/7, purchasable from the convenience of your personal computer, and the legal provider says the product is region-locked, will come to your country 3 months after the US release, and can only be purchased at a brick and mortar store, then the pirate's service is more valuable."

- Gabe Newell
